Store delivery boundary

Your store photo is a reference, not a georeferenced map. Trace the actual outline on the interactive map using taps or clicks. The boundary will stay visible over your heat map.

Open the Map tab to draw points after tapping “Start tracing”. Tap the map to add corners in order.

Map locations

To calculate geographic averages, addresses must be turned into coordinates. This demo can use OpenStreetMap's public geocoder with a strict rate limit. Using it sends selected street addresses to a third-party service. Do not use this for bulk imports or if your employer prohibits sharing delivery addresses.
